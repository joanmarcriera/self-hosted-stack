SME Automation Demonstration

A modern operating stack for small companies.

This public site fronts a reproducible self-hosted stack for a three-person automation consultancy. It is designed to show how lead intake, delivery coordination, documentation, workflows, and monitoring can fit together without turning into a heavyweight enterprise maze.

Example delivery loop

A client request should become a documented, monitorable delivery system with as little friction as possible.

1
A lead lands in EspoCRM and triggers an n8n workflow for intake, routing, or follow-up.
2
Discovery notes, runbooks, and handover material are written in BookStack instead of disappearing in chat.
3
Tasks move in Vikunja while Grafana tracks service health and future funnel telemetry.
